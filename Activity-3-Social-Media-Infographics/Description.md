# Activity 3 — Social Media Infographics and Mini Project Documentation

## For this activity, we were instructed to create an infographic that presented a problem and proposed a creative solution using visual communication. My infographic, titled “One Wrong Step: The Risk of Patient Falls,” focused on the risks and consequences of patient falls in hospital settings. I developed the concept around the importance of identifying and addressing fall risks, with my proposed solution being “Addressing Fall Risks in Hospital Settings Through a Digital Patient Fall-Risk Monitoring System.” I organized the information into a visually understandable format by combining concise text, graphics, colors, icons, and other design elements to make the topic easier to follow. The activity also involved documenting the creative process and explaining the concept and design choices behind the final infographic. Through this project, I was able to apply presentation and visual design principles to a healthcare-related topic while communicating a practical solution in an engaging way.
