# Activity 2 — Color Palette and Typography

## For this activity, we were instructed to create our own personal branding while focusing on the use of color palette, typography, and overall visual identity. I created a fictional gelato brand called Kassélime Gelato, inspired by my appreciation for gelato and my experience traveling in Italy. I developed a warm and elegant color palette using cream, yellow, golden yellow, and dark brown, while incorporating different fonts to establish a clear visual hierarchy and give the brand its own personality. I then applied these branding elements to designs such as a business card to see how consistently the colors and typography could be used across different materials. The activity allowed me to explore how design choices can work together to create a recognizable and cohesive brand identity.
