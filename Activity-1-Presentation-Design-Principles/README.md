For this activity, we were instructed to create a promotional poster for a booth in Arrupe Hall that was offering free coffee. We were given specific details that needed to be included in the poster, and I developed a coffee-themed concept that would make the promotion look warm, inviting, and eye-catching. I applied different presentation design principles such as contrast, repetition, proximity, emphasis, and visual hierarchy to organize the information and highlight the most important details. I also selected appropriate colors, fonts, images, and decorative elements to support the coffee theme while keeping the poster easy to understand. The final output was designed to communicate the promotion quickly while maintaining a visually appealing and organized layout.
